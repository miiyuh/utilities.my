# Code of Conduct

utilities.my is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (opening issues, reviewing pull requests, suggesting features or just asking questions) deserves to be treated with care. This is how we work together.

## Our commitment

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** People come with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Explain, don't belittle.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- **Give feedback on the work, not the person.** Be specific and constructive, and be gracious when receiving it.
- **Respect people's time and privacy.** Keep discussions on topic, and never share someone else's personal information.
- **Own your mistakes.** Apologise to anyone affected and learn from it.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing others' private information (such as a home or email address) without their explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption
- Encouraging any of the above

## Where this applies

This code applies in every project space: the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits) and anywhere you represent the project publicly.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, please tell the maintainer privately through [miiyuh.com](https://miiyuh.com). You can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature on any issue, comment or pull request.

Every report will be read and handled promptly and confidentially. The privacy and safety of the person reporting comes first.

Security vulnerabilities are different: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead, so they can be fixed before they are made public.

## How it's enforced

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may:

1. **Correct**: a private note explaining what was wrong, and a request to do better.
2. **Warn**: a clear warning, with consequences for further behaviour.
3. **Temporarily ban**: no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanently ban**: for serious or repeated violations.

Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code may be edited, hidden or removed.
